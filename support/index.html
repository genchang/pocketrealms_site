My steps aren't counting

Pocket Realms reads your steps from Apple Health, so it needs permission to read Steps.

  • On your Apple Watch: Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Pocket Realms, and turn on Steps.
  • Or on your iPhone: Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Pocket Realms.
  • In the game, Settings › Health access shows whether steps are coming in. If you've never been asked, it has an "allow step access" button.

Steps can take a minute to arrive from your iPhone. Open the game again and they'll catch up.

How do chests work?

Treasure chests appear as you walk: the first at 500 steps, then at 1,500, 3,000, 5,000 and 7,500. That's five a day. Tap a chest to open it: it holds coins or a find for your backpack. Chests count your whole day's steps, so walking without the app open still counts. Chests left unopened at the end of the day are opened for you, for coins, the next time you open the game.

What is the cobblestone town?

A second place to walk: a cobblestone path, a river with a stone bridge, and a castle on the hill. Unlock it on the shop's "saving up" page with 2,000 coins, earned by walking. Your camp, finds and coins are the same in every realm; switch between them in Settings › Realm.

How do I start again?

Settings › Reset progress, then confirm. It clears coins, chests, finds, camp decor and unlocked characters, and takes you back to the meadow. Today's steps still count, so today's chests come back as you walk.

Where is my data?

On your Apple Watch, and nowhere else. Pocket Realms has no account, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. See the privacy policy.

How do I add the widget?

On your watch face, turn the Digital Crown to open the Smart Stack, then touch and hold it and tap +. Choose Pocket Realms to see how many steps are left until your next chest.
