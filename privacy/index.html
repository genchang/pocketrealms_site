Privacy Policy

Pocket Realms: Step Quest · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Pocket Realms is a step-counting adventure game for Apple Watch, made by Genevieve Chang. This policy explains what the app uses and what happens to it. In short: everything stays on your watch. The one exception is the optional cobblestone town purchase, which is handled by Apple.

What the app reads

  • Your step count, from Apple Health. With your permission, Pocket Realms reads today's step total so your character can walk and find treasure chests, and so the widget can show how close your next chest is. It only reads steps. It never writes to Health and never reads any other kind of health data.

What the app stores

  • Your game progress: coins, chests, finds, camp decor, characters and realms you've unlocked, and your settings. It's saved on your Apple Watch, so the game and its widgets can show it.
  • Whether you've bought the cobblestone town: a small note on your watch, so the town shows straight away when the app opens. The App Store stays the record of your purchase.
  • That's all. There is no account and no sign-in.

What the app doesn't do

  • It doesn't send your step count, your progress or anything else to us or to anyone else. The app has no servers of its own. Its only connection is to Apple's App Store, through Apple's in-app purchase system, to show the town's price, to buy or restore the town, and to check whether you've bought it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services or SDKs.
  • Health data is never used for advertising and never sold or shared.

Purchases

Pocket Realms has one optional in-app purchase: the cobblestone town, a second place to walk. It's a one-time purchase, and you can also unlock the town with coins earned by walking. Coins are never sold.

  • Buying and restoring are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.
  • The app only learns from Apple whether you own the town. It checks this when it opens, and when you buy or restore.
  • Your purchase is kept by Apple, not in your game progress. Resetting your progress or reinstalling the app doesn't lose it; use Settings → Restore purchases to get it back on a new install.

Your choices

  • Health access: you can turn step access off or on at any time. On your Apple Watch, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Health › Pocket Realms. Without it, the game can't count your steps.
  • Delete your progress: use Settings → Reset progress in the app, or delete the app. Deleting it removes everything it stored on your watch. A purchase stays with your Apple Account.

Children

Pocket Realms doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and an app update will point to it. If the app ever starts collecting data, it will ask you first.
